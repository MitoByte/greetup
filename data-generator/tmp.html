About us

Beers with Peers Tap Talks is a group for information technology and digital transformation professionals or, anyone interested in a career in technology. We get together for good company and conversation. Mostly we talk information technology but sometimes we talk marketing technology/ craft breweries / other fun events. Please come and relax a bit after work with us. Beers with Peers Tap Talks is a lot of easygoing fun and a great place to meet peers in the industry and make new connections! Our sponsored Tap Talks include a wide array of specialties - all are welcome! Infrastructure * Cyber Security * Software* Data * Cloud * Creative (SEO - Graphic Design - Marketing) * Digital Transformation * Business Liaison (Project Management, Business Analysts, Product Manager, Scrum Master), the list goes on! SPONSORSHIP OPTIONS ARE AVAILABLE AND WELCOME - PLEASE CONTACT DAVE WOODWARD FOR DETAILS! Beers with Peers meets bi-monthly at a different exceptional craft brewery located in SE Wisconsin. It is an evening of casual networking, an informative Tap Talk, free craft beer and excellent food. Your advance registration via our Google form (the registration link is shown on every BwP LinkedIn event's page) will sign you up to enter a drawing for raffle prizes. Your first two drinks are free with your advance registration!

    The Importance of #Third-#Party #Risk #Management in #Cybersecurity In today’s #interconnected world, #businesses often rely on third-party #vendors for #essential #services, from #cloud #storage to #software #solutions. While this provides #valuable #efficiencies and #capabilities, it also opens the door to #significant cybersecurity #risks. Third-party risk management (#TPRM) has become a #critical #component of any #robust cybersecurity #strategy. Here's why: ✔️ #Vulnerabilities in the #Supply #Chain Each third-party relationship introduces a new potential #entry #point for #cyber #threats. A #vulnerability in one vendor's #system can become an #attack #vector that #jeopardizes your entire #network. ✔️ #Shared #Responsibility Your security #posture doesn’t end at your #firewall. It extends to the vendors you work with. Ensuring they meet your cybersecurity #standards is vital to minimizing risks to your #data, #reputation, and #compliance. ✔️ #Proactive Risk #Assessment Third-party risk management isn’t just about reactive measures—it's about proactively #identifying risks through due #diligence, regular #audits, and #monitoring. This helps #mitigate #threats before they become #breaches. ✔️ #Regulatory Compliance Many #industries are now required to #assess third-party risks as part of compliance standards (such as #GDPR, #HIPAA, and others). A strong #TPRM #program can help your organization #avoid #costly #fines and #penalties. To #build a #resilient cybersecurity #strategy, it’s #essential to: ◼️ #Assess and monitor the security posture of third-party vendors #regularly. ◼️ Set #clear security #expectations and #require #adherence to #best #practices. ◼️ #Implement #multi-#layered #defense #mechanisms to mitigate risks. In the end, cybersecurity is a shared responsibility. #Ensuring that third parties meet your security standards is one of the best ways to #protect #your #business from evolving cyber threats. Digassurance.com #CyberSecurity #RiskManagement #ThirdPartyRisk #SupplyChainSecurity #InfoSec #DataProtection #Compliance #CyberResilience #DAA #DigitalAssurance
